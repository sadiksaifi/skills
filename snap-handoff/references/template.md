# Handoff Template

Use concise bullets. Omit optional sections that add no continuation value.

<template>

# Handoff

## Goal

[Desired outcome and current scope.]

## Status

- Completed: [finished work]
- In progress: [current work and exact stopping point]
- Blocked: [blocker, or `None`]

## Decisions and constraints

- [Decision, rationale, consequence, or user preference.]

## References

- `[path, URL, branch, commit, issue, or PR]` - [why it matters]

## Verification

- `[command or check]` - [result]

## Next

1. [First executable action.]
2. [Following action.]

## Open questions

- [Unresolved decision and why it matters.]

</template>

`Goal`, `Status`, and `Next` are required. Omit empty optional sections rather than writing placeholders such as `None`; `Blocked: None` is the only explicit empty state.
