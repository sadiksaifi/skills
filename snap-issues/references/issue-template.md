# Issue Template

Use a short, specific outcome title. The summary explains why the issue exists and where its scope ends. The acceptance criteria are the single source of required behavior for humans and agents.

Keep the summary to one or two sentences without restating the checklist. Use two to five observable acceptance checks. Include only context that changes implementation, and prefer a canonical source link over repeated background.

<template>

## Summary

[Explain why this issue exists and its scope boundary in one or two sentences. Leave required behavior to the checklist.]

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] [Primary externally verifiable behavior.]
- [ ] [Material error, permission, or edge behavior.]

## Context

- Source: [Canonical issue, plan, specification, or discussion.]
- Constraint: [Decision or contract the implementer must preserve.]

</template>

Omit `## Context` when there is no necessary source, decision, or constraint.
